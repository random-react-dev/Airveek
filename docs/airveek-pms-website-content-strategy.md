# Airveek PMS Website and Content Strategy

**Date:** 27 June 2026  
**Product:** Airveek Hotel PMS  
**Primary audience:** Independent hotels and accommodation properties

## 1. Executive Summary

Airveekની visual foundation સારી છે, પરંતુ industry-level PMS marketing website બનાવવા માટે ચાર બાબતો સૌથી મહત્વની છે:

- Real product proof
- Specific target positioning
- Verified customer trust
- Clear demo and pricing conversion flow

Airveekએ Cloudbeds અથવા Mews જેવી enterprise breadth claim ન કરવી જોઈએ. તેનું strongest positioning independent hotels, Indian payment workflows, simple daily operations અને guided onboarding છે.

Recommended core positioning:

> A practical hotel PMS for independent hotels, built around real front-desk, payment and housekeeping workflows.

Supporting message:

> Airveek brings reservations, rooms, guests, payments, housekeeping and reports into one clear workspace, with guided setup for your property and team.

1. Specific target audience
2. Outcome-focused hero
3. Real product screenshots
4. Early trust signals
5. Benefits before feature inventory

8. Pricing direction

## 3. Recommended Airveek Positioning

### Primary positioning

> A practical hotel PMS for independent hotels, built around real front-desk, payment and housekeeping workflows.

### Supporting positioning

> Airveek brings reservations, rooms, guests, payments, housekeeping and reports into one clear workspace, with guided setup for your property and team.

### Primary differentiators

- Independent hotels and lean teams
- Cash and UPI workflows
- Guided setup and onboarding
- Simple front-desk operations
- Property-specific flexibility
- Built from actual hotel operations
- Roles and operational accountability
- Local support potential

### How to position customization

“Custom software”ને primary promise ન બનાવવો. Unlimited customization scalability અને maintenance અંગે doubt ઊભો કરી શકે છે.

Recommended wording:

> Need a property-specific workflow? We scope approved customizations separately after understanding your operations.

## 4. Recommended Homepage Structure

### Section 1: Sticky Navigation

Recommended navigation:

- Product
- Features
- Solutions
- Pricing
- Resources
- Support

Recommended actions:

- Login
- Book a Demo

Rules:

- Dead anchors ન હોવા જોઈએ.
- Unavailable product pages navigationમાં ન બતાવવી.
- Demo CTA actual form, calendar અથવા contact flow સાથે જોડાયેલ હોવો જોઈએ.

### Section 2: Hero

Purpose: પાંચ secondsમાં product, target customer અને business value સમજાવવી.

Recommended content:

**Eyebrow**

> Hotel PMS for independent properties

**Headline**

> Run your hotel from one clear system.

**Description**

> Airveek brings reservations, rooms, guests, payments, housekeeping and reports into one practical workspace, so your team always knows what needs attention next.

**Primary CTA**

> Book a Free Demo

**Secondary CTA**

> Explore the Product

The hero should eventually contain a real dashboard screenshot or a short product video. Generic hotel stock photography should not be the primary visual.

### Section 3: Immediate Trust Strip

Do not use fake numbers or unverified OTA partnership logos.

Verified trust points can include:

- Built from real hotel operations
- Guided property setup
- Role-based team access
- Cash and UPI workflows

Customer count, time saved or revenue improvement should only be displayed after the numbers are verified.

### Section 4: Product Proof

Recommended heading:

> Everything your team needs to run the day, connected.

Recommended workflow tabs:

- Dashboard
- Calendar
- Reservations
- Payments
- Housekeeping
- Reports

Every tab should contain:

- Outcome-focused title
- Workflow description
- Three concise capabilities
- Real product screenshot

Sensitive guest names, phone numbers, payment references and private financial data must be removed or masked in screenshots.

### Section 5: Problem and Outcome

Recommended heading:

> Hotel work becomes difficult when every team uses a different system.

Problems to communicate:

- Bookings spreadsheets and messagesમાં scattered રહે છે
- Room readiness front deskને સમયસર ખબર પડતી નથી
- Payments bookings સાથે match કરવી મુશ્કેલ બને છે
- Ownersને daily occupancy and revenue clarity મળતી નથી
- Staff access and responsibility unclear રહે છે
- Manual work વધતાં operational mistakes વધે છે

After showing these problems, immediately explain how Airveek creates one shared operational view.

### Section 6: Core PMS Workflow Pillars

Avoid presenting 12-15 equal feature cards. Group the product into six meaningful workflows.

#### Reservations and Front Desk

Create bookings, manage guest details, room assignments, check-ins and check-outs.

#### Rooms and Rates

Manage room types, physical rooms, availability, rates and closures.

#### Payments and Billing

Record cash and UPI payments, track balances and manage invoice workflows.

#### Housekeeping

Share room readiness between reception and housekeeping.

#### Reports and Owner View

Monitor bookings, occupancy, revenue, collections and pending dues.

#### Roles and Accountability

Control staff access and maintain activity visibility.

### Section 7: Payments and Indian Hotel Workflows

This can become one of Airveek's strongest differentiators.

Recommended features:

- Cash payment recording
- UPI and QR workflows
- Paid, pending and unmatched payment visibility
- Payment attachment
- Outstanding balances
- Invoice and receipt workflows
- Credit notes, where production-ready

Recommended heading:

> Know what is paid, pending and still needs attention.

Avoid:

> Fully GST compliant

Use careful wording until tax workflows are legally verified:

> Configurable invoice and GST-ready billing workflows.

### Section 8: Housekeeping and Room Readiness

Recommended heading:

> Keep every room ready for the next arrival.

Content:

- Clean
- Dirty
- Inspected
- Maintenance
- Room readiness visibility
- Front desk and housekeeping coordination

If Airveek does not have a native mobile application, do not use “Mobile app.” Use:

> Mobile-friendly access for hotel teams.

### Section 9: Reports and Owner Visibility

Recommended capabilities:

- Occupancy
- Revenue
- Arrivals and departures
- Booking performance
- Payment collections
- Pending dues
- Booking sources
- Exportable reports, if available

Recommended heading:

> Give owners answers without rebuilding reports.

### Section 10: Guided Setup and Migration

Switching anxiety is a major PMS buying objection. The website should explain exactly how Airveek reduces migration risk.

Recommended process:

1. Property and workflow review
2. Rooms and rates setup
3. Staff roles configuration
4. Existing booking and data migration
5. Team training
6. Go-live support

Recommended heading:

> Move to Airveek without disrupting daily operations.

Only promise migration after an actual, repeatable migration process is available.

### Section 11: Property Types

Keep the primary target segments focused:

- Independent hotels
- Boutique hotels
- Guest houses
- Resorts
- Wellness stays
- Service apartments

Each important property type can later have a dedicated SEO landing page.

Example:

> Airveek for boutique hotels  
> Manage personalised stays, room readiness and guest details without enterprise complexity.

### Section 12: Comparison

Avoid unsupported statements such as “Generic PMS is slow or expensive.”

Use a practical comparison:

> Manual operations vs Airveek

Recommended rows:

- Booking visibility
- Room readiness
- Payment tracking
- Owner reporting
- Staff access
- Operational accountability

Recommended heading:

> What changes when your team works from one PMS.

Named competitor comparisons should be placed on separate SEO pages and supported by verifiable facts.

### Section 13: Pricing

If final pricing has not been confirmed, do not show fake Starter, Growth and Custom plans.

Recommended message:

> Flexible pricing based on your room count and required modules.

Pricing should explain:

- Core PMS inclusion
- Number of users
- Guided setup
- Training
- Standard support
- Setup or migration fee
- Optional integrations
- Custom workflow charges

Recommended CTA:

> Get My Property Quote

Recommended form fields:

- Property name
- City
- Property type
- Room count
- Current booking method or PMS
- Required modules
- Phone or WhatsApp
- Preferred demo time

### Section 14: Customer Case Study

Sahajanand Wellness can be used as product-origin proof after receiving client permission.

Recommended heading:

> Built from real hotel operations.

Recommended content:

> Airveek's first operational workflows were shaped with Sahajanand Wellness across reservations, room management, payments, housekeeping and reporting.

A useful case study should contain:

- Client logo
- Real product screenshot
- Customer quote
- One verified operational result
- Property type and room count

Never invent percentages or operational results.

### Section 15: Integrations

Only show integrations that are live and verified.

Recommended integration categories:

- Payments
- Accounting
- Communication
- Booking channels
- Operational tools

If OTA connectivity is not live, do not use Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia or Airbnb logos as trust signals. These logos can imply certified integration or an official partnership.

### Section 16: Security and Data Control

Only show verified controls:

- Role-based access
- Activity logs
- Property-level data isolation
- Secure document access
- Backup policy
- Payment security
- Data ownership

“Property isolation roadmap” is not a security feature. Multi-tenant isolation should only be marketed after it is production-ready and tested.

### Section 17: FAQ

Recommended questions:

- What type of properties is Airveek built for?
- What is included in setup?
- Can Airveek migrate existing bookings?
- Can staff access Airveek from mobile devices?
- Does Airveek support cash and UPI payments?
- Is pricing based on room count?
- Can we request property-specific workflows?
- Does Airveek connect with OTAs?
- How is hotel data separated and protected?
- What support is available after onboarding?

Answers should be short, honest and customer-facing. Internal strategy language must never appear in the public FAQ.

### Section 18: Final CTA

Recommended heading:

> See how Airveek fits your hotel workflow.

Recommended description:

> Tell us about your rooms, booking process, payments and staff operations. We'll show you how Airveek can bring them into one clear system.

Recommended actions:

- Book a Free Demo
- Talk on WhatsApp

The CTA must connect to a real lead form, scheduling calendar or WhatsApp contact.

## 7. Content Tone

### Preferred language

- Manage bookings
- Track room readiness
- Match payments
- Know today's arrivals
- Train your team
- Monitor pending dues
- Run daily operations

### Language to avoid

- Revolutionise hospitality
- Next-generation ecosystem
- AI-powered transformation
- Complete automation
- Seamless OTA synchronization, until live
- Fully GST compliant, until legally verified
- Enterprise-grade security, without evidence or certification

The content should remain practical, operational and specific.

## 8. Visual and UX Direction

- Use real product screenshots rather than generic SaaS mockups.
- Mask sensitive guest and payment information.
- Keep Airveek orange as the primary action color.
- Use status colors only where they communicate operational meaning.
- Avoid excessive cards and oversized rounded containers.
- Keep product screenshots large enough to read.
- Use horizontal tabs only when all options remain discoverable on mobile.
- Do not render fake screenshots or empty placeholder messages.
- Ensure every CTA has a working destination.
- Keep mobile layouts usable for hotel owners and staff on the move.

## 9. Primary Conversion Flow

Every major CTA should lead to one of two actions:

1. Book a demo
2. Contact Airveek through WhatsApp or a lead form

Recommended lead information:

- Name
- Phone or WhatsApp
- Email
- Hotel or property name
- City
- Number of rooms
- Current booking method or PMS
- Main operational problem
- Preferred demo time

After submission, clearly explain the next step:

> Our team will contact you to understand your rooms, booking sources, payment process and staff workflow before the product demonstration.

## 11. Final Recommendation

Airveek's strongest marketing angle is:

> A practical PMS for independent hotels, built from real operations and supported with guided property setup.

The website should sell operational clarity rather than feature quantity. Real screenshots, honest feature claims, a focused target audience, guided onboarding and a working demo flow will create more trust than broad enterprise-style claims.
